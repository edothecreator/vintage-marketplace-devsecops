 GOAL
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 Close the DevSecOps loop. Up to now everything has been pre-deployment;
 P5 Continuous monitoring is a Tier 1 practice covering the Predict and Detect
 lifecycle phases, i.e. security AFTER release.

 Without this phase the pipeline stops at deployment and four lifecycle phases
 (Release, Predict, Respond, Detect) have no coverage.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 NOTES
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 - The strongest evidence here is an alert that actually fired. Run a brute
   force against your own staging login, show the spike, show the alert. That
   is far more convincing than a dashboard of flat zeroes.

 - Metrics endpoints are a classic information-disclosure mistake. ASVS 13.4.5
   requires monitoring endpoints not be exposed unless intended. Verify from
   outside the Docker network.
